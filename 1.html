2.1 Definition of Topology

Network topology is the arrangement of computers and other devices in a network. It explains how devices are connected and how they communicate with each other.

Example: Computers connected to one central switch in an office form a Star Topology.

Quick Revision

Bus: One main cable.

Star: One central switch or hub.

Ring: Devices connected in a loop.

Mesh: Multiple paths between devices.

Hybrid: Combination of topologies.

Wi-Fi: Wireless local networking.

Satellite: Communication using satellites.

Radio Waves: Wireless signal transmission.
